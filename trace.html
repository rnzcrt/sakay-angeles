Trace a route

Lines you draw here are illustrative drafts. They are shown dashed in the app and are never used for trip suggestions until stops and directions are verified.
  1. Pick a route, then Direction A or B.
  2. Your first click is the START (the terminal, e.g. Chevalier) and your last click is the END. Trace along the road in the order the jeepney travels. No typing needed.
  3. Click along the road. With Snap to roads on, the path follows the streets (including one-way rules) between your clicks.
  4. Drag a point to move it; double-click a point to delete it.
  5. If the way back uses a different road, trace it as Direction B.
  6. Press Export, then replace public/data/routes.geojson.
