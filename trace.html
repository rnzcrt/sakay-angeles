Trace a route

Lines you draw here are illustrative drafts. They are shown dashed in the app and are never used for trip suggestions until stops and directions are verified.
  1. Pick a route.
  2. Route 1 — going: your first click is the first terminal. Click along the road to the end. The last click is the new terminal — Route 2 starts there.
  3. Route 2 — coming back: press Start Route 2 where Route 1 ends, click along the road back (it may be a different road), then press Finish Route 2 at the start of Route 1.
  4. With Snap to roads on, the path follows the streets (including one-way rules) between your clicks. If it takes a long detour, it is replaced by a straight line and you are told. Hold Option/Alt while clicking to add a straight (unsnapped) piece, or turn snapping off and click at every corner.
  5. Drag a point to move it; double-click a point to delete it. Click a point to select it, then “Cut after the selected point” to throw away everything after it and keep tracing from there.
  6. Preview the traced lines on the map, then use Save route geometry to publish the corrected geometry with a change record.
